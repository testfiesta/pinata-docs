# Reset Password



<details>

<summary>Step 1: Click "Forgot your Password?" </summary>

At the log in page, click "Forgot your Password?" right above the "Log in" button. This will take you to the recovery page.

</details>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/forgot password.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<details>

<summary>Step 2: Type in your email and click "Send email" </summary>

This will send a password recovery email to your inbox (check the spam folder).&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>Step 3: Click the recovery link from the email</summary>

Clicking the link will bring you back to Piñata where you will be able to change your password.&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>Step 4: Change your Password</summary>

Once you have click the recovery email, you will be able to change your password. Once you are finished making your new password, you will have to confirm it by typing it again. Then click "Create new Password"&#x20;

</details>

<details>

<summary>Step 5: Log in with new Password</summary>

Once you have successfully changed your password, you can once again log in.&#x20;

</details>
